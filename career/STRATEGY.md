# Role and compensation strategy

## Starting recommendation

Target senior backend engineer and hands-on tech lead roles in three parallel groups: banking/GCC engineering teams, payment/fintech product teams, and broader backend product teams. The learner's Java/Spring/SQL and BFSI experience is relevant; the diagnostic will establish depth and level. Do not restrict the search to roles with the exact current title.

This is a fit hypothesis supported by the [research ledger](../research/SOURCES.md), not a claim about guaranteed offers or pay. Staff engineer and engineering manager are not default targets: they require evidence of the corresponding scope or people-management responsibilities. Employer levels differ.

| Group | Evidence to emphasize | What to confirm with recruiter |
|---|---|---|
| Banking / financial-services GCC | Transaction correctness, SQL, delivery controls, production ownership, domain depth | Coding depth, role scope, title mapping, on-call, compensation band |
| Fintech / payments product | Failure handling, APIs, data integrity, scale, coding and design | DSA/machine coding format, ownership expectations, fixed/variable split |
| Broader backend product | Transferable design reasoning, coding, reliability, technical leadership | Level calibration, language constraints, interview loop, compensation structure |

Examples in the research are role-scope evidence, not a curated list of employers paying >=40 LPA. Start a shortlist only after geography, compensation definition, and role constraints are known. Verify live openings when applying.

## INR 40+ LPA target

Clarify whether this means **annual fixed compensation** or **total CTC**. Until clarified, keep both columns and make no pass/fail salary judgment. Separate guaranteed recurring cash, target variable pay, employer contributions/benefits, one-time joining payments, and annual vesting equity. Do not compare headline multi-year grants with annual fixed cash. Ask about bonus conditions, vesting, cliffs, clawbacks, and review cycles before deciding whether an offer meets the learner's objective.

No unsupported salary range is assigned to an employer. Ask recruiters for the approved band early enough to avoid interviewing for an incompatible role. Evaluate role quality, growth, location, workload, and risk alongside compensation. Do not invent competing offers, current compensation, responsibilities, or resume metrics.

## Career work within the weekly budget

Weeks 1–2: document two real systems, personal contributions, constraints, scale evidence, incidents, and architecture decisions. Draft a concise positioning statement: background, ownership, impact, and desired next scope.

Weeks 3–4: turn genuine experience into resume bullets and six story outlines: ownership, incident, disagreement, failure, mentoring, and delivery tradeoff. Metrics must be real or explicitly described as unavailable. Redact proprietary details.

Weeks 5–6: build a target list from live roles, map gaps to the curriculum, rehearse a five-minute project explanation, and prepare questions for the hiring manager. Conduct lower-stakes interviews if the learner chooses, with a debrief after each.

Weeks 7–8: adapt mock loops to real interview formats, refine stories, check role/compensation fit, and rehearse negotiation language. Sending applications, referral messages, or recruiter replies requires explicit learner instruction.

## Project deep-dive worksheet

For each real project, record: business problem; system boundaries; personal role; actual traffic/data volume if known; request/data flow; schema; consistency requirements; failure modes; largest bottleneck; rejected alternatives; testing/release approach; one incident; measurable impact; what you would change now. Keep confidential employer data outside this repo, using anonymous summaries.

## Opportunity tracker

Use [opportunities.csv](opportunities.csv). Add real rows only when a job or conversation exists. A blank tracker is more honest than a fabricated shortlist. Record job URL, confirmed interview format, recruiter-stated compensation, and next action. Do not place credentials or private recruiter contact details in a public repository.
